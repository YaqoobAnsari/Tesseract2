Route fidelity: graph routes vs free-space geodesics

FF part 1upE: 98 pairs (88 scored vs full free space, 88 vs interior-only)
  stretch vs FULL free space (permissive lower bound):     mean 1.047, median 1.037, p90 1.093
  stretch vs INTERIOR-ONLY (conservative reference):       mean 1.047, median 1.037, p90 1.093
  geodesic/euclid: mean 1.665, p90 2.210, max 3.333

FF part 2up: 380 pairs (359 scored vs full free space, 124 vs interior-only)
  stretch vs FULL free space (permissive lower bound):     mean 2.300, median 1.890, p90 4.211
  stretch vs INTERIOR-ONLY (conservative reference):       mean 1.237, median 1.106, p90 1.552
  geodesic/euclid: mean 1.348, p90 1.639, max 3.178

Reading: the two references bracket the truth. The FULL free-space
geodesic is permissive (may shortcut through semantically outdoor
space); the INTERIOR-ONLY geodesic is conservative (excludes the
pipeline's semantic outside class, and cannot score pairs whose
only connection runs outdoors). Graph route fidelity lies between
the two stretch numbers. 'geodesic/euclid' well above 1 shows why
straight-line Euclidean is a weak reference (walls force real
detours). Overlays under overlays/ show geodesic (orange dots) vs
graph route (blue line) for author verification.
